Does Africa Have the Skills to Compete in the AI Era?

People who can build, adapt and govern AI matter as much as cables and data centres.

There is no reliable, comparable measure of AI skills for all African countries. The indicators below are proxies: they describe education and research capacity in general, not AI talent specifically.
