Can Africa Build the Infrastructure for AI?

AI needs electricity, connectivity and devices before it needs algorithms.

Here we ask whether countries with stronger digital infrastructure also tend to score higher on AI readiness. A strong association would still not prove that one causes the other: both depend on income and other factors.
