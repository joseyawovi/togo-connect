What is inside africa.json

  • countries — the 54 countries, same fields as the CSV.
  • series — year-by-year World Bank time series (internet, electricity, mobile, tertiary) used by the line charts.
  • globalLeaders — the top-scoring countries worldwide, used in the “Africa compared with global leaders” chart.
  • indicators — indicator names and World Bank codes shown in chart captions.

Original sources

  • Oxford Insights, Government AI Readiness Index 2025
  • World Bank Open Data (via the World Development Indicators API)

Values were downloaded from these public sources and lightly cleaned for this project. Reference years differ between countries and indicators, which is why each value carries its own year.
