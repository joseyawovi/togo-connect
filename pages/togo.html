Opportunities and structural challenges

The opportunities below are possible uses of AI discussed in the research literature, not measured results for Togo.

  • Agriculture: farming employs a large share of workers in Togo, so tools for weather, crop disease and market prices could reach many people.
  • Education: digital learning tools could support teachers, but they depend on electricity and connectivity in schools.
  • Healthcare: decision-support tools could help where there are few doctors per person.
  • Public services: AI could make digital government services more efficient.

What the data shows

  • Togo's AI readiness score is close to the African average and slightly above Burkina Faso's, but below Ghana's and Benin's.
  • Access to electricity and the internet is still incomplete, which limits any AI use that depends on them.
  • Togo reports few researchers (about 58 per million people) and low R&D spending (about 0.2% of GDP), so its research capacity is still small.
